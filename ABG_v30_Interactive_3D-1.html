Main stages of ABG-v30

  1. Initialization: Sobol design with an optional center point.
  2. Subdivision: Select a region by its priority score and split along its widest dimension.
  3. Exploration: Periodically select an older region and audit regions.
  4. Proposal: Evaluate a surrogate proposal only if it passes validation.
  5. Refinement: Multi-start L-BFGS-B and ring search under a shared budget.
